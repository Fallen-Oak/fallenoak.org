I am pleased to announce Magnum is back!

This 17’2 plus Oldenburg out of Art Deco is one of my favorite rescues of all time. Talk about personality! This guy will be 21 in April and has earned sanctuary for the rest of his days. Magnum came from Ohio almost three years ago after a serious suspensory injury ended his eventing career.

After lots of rehab, he was deemed sound for light riding and was adopted within days. Magnum's prior adopter returned him after two members of his family became seriously ill, and all of his attention needed to be focused on helping to care for them.

The rescue will always take back a horse that has been adopted if life circumstances change. It is part of our contract. I am thrilled Magnum is home once again and will remain at my personal farm for the rest of his days. He'll enjoy riding from time to time by my daughter Trinity, who is thrilled he’s back.

Welcome home Mags, a truly gentle giant.
