Beauty, aka Phantom Girl, is a 15-year-old off the track thoroughbred. Beauty stands 16’3, is currently barefoot, and is up-to-date on everything.

Beauty is an incredible mover, has potential for jumping is ridden both English and western, but would love to see her go to an English riding home, where she can excel and be shown. Beauty was returned by a prior adopter after 6 weeks, as she was too much horse for her 8-year-old.

Beauty is fine for a child as long as they are a confident rider. Beauty is currently located at our trainers in Ocala. We have tons of riding videos we can send you. If you would like to come see her, please reach out. Let’s find this sweet girl her forever home. Adoption fee is $1,200.
