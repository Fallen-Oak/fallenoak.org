Allow me to introduce the new guy, Si! This sweet boy came to us from a wonderful home, his mama did individual rescue for years. I was privileged to haul a couple of horses Joy rehabbed to cooler climates.

Si’s owner is moving north and no longer doing rescue she needed a soft place for him to spend the rest of his days. Si is a 25-year-old TN walker. He is the kindest soul, Trinity my daughter keeps him in shape by riding him bareback around the farm, they both thoroughly enjoy it.

I’m glad I could help someone who has helped many horses throughout the years, it is so important we help each other in the rescue world when we can. Welcome to your forever home, Si!
