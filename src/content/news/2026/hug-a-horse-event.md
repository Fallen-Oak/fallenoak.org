Join us on February 7th, from 11am - 3pm at Fallen Oak Equine Rescue, for the Hug a Horse Valentine’s Event, a heartfelt, hands-on day celebrating second chances and the impact of proper nutrition and rehabilitation.

Fallen Oak is known for stepping up for horses others can’t, giving each one the time and care they need to heal and rebuild trust.

Hosted in partnership with Triple Crown Feed, J&J Tack Shack, and Ian Robb with BEMER.

What to enjoy:

Treat Station:

“Buy” treats to give to the rescue horses in exchange for a donation including mints and Triple Crown Functional Wafers.

BEMER Info Station:

Learn about BEMER therapy from Ian Robb!

Pick a Valentine!

Shop at the J&J Tack Shack pop-up booth purchase feed, hay, shavings, and more to support the rescue horses.

College of Central Florida Student Demonstration:

Pre-vet students And Equine Student from the College of Central Florida will demonstrate how to take TPR (temperature, pulse, respiration) and identify the external parts of the horse.

The address for the event is 1 SE 123rd Street Rd, Ocala, FL 34480.

For more Information, call 240-440-0807.
