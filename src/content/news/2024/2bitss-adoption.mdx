I wanted to let everyone know that 2Bits the Mustang was adopted.

There was a great deal of people interested in this sweet boy. After careful consideration, he was placed at a wonderful farm to be a lesson horse for kids. It was 2Bits's prior owner's wish that he would become a lesson horse and it came to fruition. I am so thankful for Brittany and her team!

I can’t wait to see how this guy flourishes. Congrats!
