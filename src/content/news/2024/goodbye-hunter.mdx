I received some sad news. Our sweet Hunter passed away.

Hunter was the first horse I have ever been able to adopt out that had a serious health issue. Hunter's cancer spread and he began losing a ton of weight, so the decision to let him go was made. Thanks so much to Georgia for loving him in his last year of life and taking such good care of him.

Run free, sweet boy.
