I have great news - our sweet girl April was adopted by her foster mom.

Patty has had April for more than a year and has rehabbed, loved on, and ridden her, and she has flourished. I am so thankful! This is the perfect home and place for April. Thanks Patty for opening your home to an older special needs horse and loving her the way you do. I appreciate you!

What a great life April will continue to have.
