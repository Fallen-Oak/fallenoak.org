Hi, ya’ll! I wanted to introduce you to one of our newest residents.

Give a warm welcome to Shear Attitude, AKA Atty. This lovely OTTB mare has had a lot of babies and several of them have done very well. Atty raced 12 times and then became a broodmare. Her prior owner could no longer afford to take care of her. Atty is a very sweet girl! She’s a bit underweight, but we will fix that. She will live out her days here at Fallen Oak as a sanctuary horse.

Welcome to the farm, sweet girl.
